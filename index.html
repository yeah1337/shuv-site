Tutorial

Prop or tripod

A tripod is nice. Your bag works fine. Get the phone upright and stable, four or five steps back.

Get in frame

Head to toes inside the dashed box, board included. Rash cannot grade what he cannot see.

Or hand it to a friend

Someone holding the phone works too. Tell them to keep still and keep you in the box.

Safety

Skate safe

  • Skateboarding carries a real risk of serious injury. You skate at your own risk.
  • Shuv is a coaching aid, not a safety system. It cannot see hazards, traffic, or the crack in the ground behind you.
  • Never look at your phone while you are riding. Set it down, skate, then check the score.
  • Wear a helmet and pads.
  • Shuv is for ages 13 and up.
