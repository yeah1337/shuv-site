Privacy

Last updated: September 2nd 2026

This policy explains what Shuv collects, why, and what you can do about it. Shuv is operated by Shuv LLC, 306 W Redwood St, Ste 201, Baltimore, MD 21201, USA ("we", "us"). If anything here is unclear, write to privacy@shuvskate.com.

1. The short version

Pose detection runs entirely on your device. Your camera feed and your video clips are processed on your phone and are not uploaded to us. We hold a small account record so your streak, scores and friends list work across sessions, plus basic diagnostics to keep the app from crashing.

2. Camera and video

Shuv needs camera access to grade a trick. While a session is running, the app keeps a rolling buffer of roughly the last thirty seconds of video in your device's memory so you can save a clip after you land. Frames are analysed on device to estimate pop, slide and height. Nothing in that buffer is transmitted to us, and the buffer is discarded when you leave the session or save over it. A clip only leaves your phone if you choose to save it to your camera roll or share it to another app, at which point that app's own terms and privacy policy apply. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings, though the app cannot grade tricks without it.

3. What we do collect

  • Account details. Your display name, an email address or third party sign in identifier, and your password in hashed form if you create one.
  • Progress data. Session scores, trick grades, streak length, freezes held, combo records, and timestamps for those sessions.
  • Social data. Your friends list and the entries you appear as on friends leaderboards. Only people you have added can see your leaderboard entry.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports and basic performance metrics.
  • Support correspondence. Anything you send us by email, kept so we can answer you.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third party advertising in Shuv, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

4. Why we use it

To run your account and keep your progress in sync, to show leaderboards to the friends you have added, to send the notifications you have opted into, to diagnose crashes and improve how grading behaves across devices, to prevent abuse and cheating, and to comply with legal obligations. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you for account and progress data, your consent for notifications and camera access, and our legitimate interests for diagnostics, security and product improvement.

5. Payments

Shuv Pro and streak freezes are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Those purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see, receive or store your card number, billing address or any other payment details.

What we do receive from the store is a purchase token, which our server checks with Apple or Google to confirm the purchase is genuine. From that we keep a record against your account of which tier you are on, when it expires, and the token itself so the same purchase cannot be used twice. That is all — there is no payment information in it.

6. Who we share it with

Service providers who host our backend, deliver notifications and collect crash reports, acting on our instructions under contract. Other Shuv users, limited to the profile name and leaderboard figures visible to friends you have added. Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose. A successor, if the business is sold, in which case we will tell you before your data moves.

7. Retention

Account and progress data is kept while your account is open. Delete your account in the app and we remove or anonymise it within 30 days, except where we must keep records for legal or accounting reasons. Diagnostic data is kept for up to 12 months. Support email is kept for up to 24 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and receive your data in a portable format. If you are in the UK or the EEA you may also complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California you may request disclosure of the categories of data we collect and ask us to delete it, and we will not discriminate against you for asking. To exercise any of this, email privacy@shuvskate.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Age limit

Shuv is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email privacy@shuvskate.com and we will delete it.

10. International transfers and security

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required we use approved transfer mechanisms such as the UK and EU standard contractual clauses. We protect data in transit and at rest with encryption and restrict internal access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the relevant regulator if a breach affects you and the law requires it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Support

Something broken, a score that makes no sense, or a request to delete your account. Write to support@shuvskate.com and we answer within two working days. Privacy and data requests go to privacy@shuvskate.com.
